  1. ICCVW
    Tile and Slide: A New Framework for Scaling NeRF from Local to Global 3D Earth Observation
    Camille Billouard, Dawa Derksen, Alexandre Constantin, and 1 more author
    In Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision (ICCV) Workshops, Oct 2025
    Abs Bib HTML PDF Code

    Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) have recently emerged as a competitive approach for 3D reconstruction from multi-date satellite imagery, but they remain constrained to small scenes because the volume of training rays required to cover larger areas exceeds available memory. We introduce Snake-NeRF, an out-of-core framework that scales NeRF to very large geographic regions on a single GPU. Our approach partitions the area of interest into non-overlapping 3D tiles and processes, at each step, only the pixels and network parameters relevant to the current tile. A novel 2x2 tile progression strategy and a segmented sampler prevent reconstruction artifacts along tile boundaries. The method achieves linear time complexity and introduces no compromise in reconstruction quality.

  1. IGARSS
    SAT-NGP: Unleashing Neural Graphics Primitives for Fast Relightable Transient-Free 3D Reconstruction From Satellite Imagery
    Camille Billouard, Dawa Derksen, Emmanuelle Sarrazin, and 1 more author
    In IGARSS 2024 - 2024 IEEE International Geoscience and Remote Sensing Symposium, 2024
    Abs DOI Bib HTML PDF Code

    Recent work has demonstrated the potential of Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) for 3D reconstruction from multi-date satellite imagery, with the ability to disentangle lighting and transient objects. However, these neural methods suffer from prohibitive training times of several tens of hours, compared to a few minutes for classical stereo-vision pipelines. We introduce SAT-NGP, which brings together an efficient sampling strategy and multi-resolution hash encoding inspired by Instant Neural Graphics Primitives. Training time is reduced to approximately 15 minutes on a single GPU without any degradation in reconstruction quality, making satellite NeRFs practically usable for the first time.
